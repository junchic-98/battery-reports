A hidden leak in solids
Nature Energy Joohyeon Noh, Kisuk Kang Sep 10, 2026 10.1038/s41560-026-02133-3

Published online: 10 September 2026; doi:10.1038/s41560-026-02133-3 Solid electrolytes have long been treated as materials that block electronic conduction while enabling fast lithium-ion transport.
